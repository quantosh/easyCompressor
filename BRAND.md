## Colors

| Token | Light | Dark | Usage |
|-------|-------|------|-------|
| `--accent` | `#536DFE` | `#8AB4F8` | Buttons, toggles ON, links, active states |
| `--accent-hover` | `#3F51B5` | `#A8C7FA` | Hover states |
| `--bg` | `#FFFFFF` | `#292A2D` | Popup background |
| `--surface` | `#F8F9FA` | `#35363A` | Panels, cards |

| `--danger` | `#D32F2F` | `#EF5350` | Disable, gain reduction badge |
